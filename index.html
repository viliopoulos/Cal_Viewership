Cal's Positioning

Three facts that frame the picture

The gap is real

Cal ranks #56 of 131 FBS programs and 13th of 17 in the ACC by 2025 viewership. The super-league brands (Alabama ~8.5M, Texas/Georgia ~7.5M) draw 10–16× our audience.

The move added reach

In the Pac-12, most Cal home games sat on the Pac-12 Network with little national footprint. In the ACC, 8 games a year are nationally rated on ESPN/ESPN2 — and several clear 1M+.

We control real levers

The late 7:30pm PT (10:30 ET) window is open on the West Coast — Cal averages 1.16M there. Add a marquee 2026 home slate, a digital audience we own, and new energy & momentum for the football program.

Cal is a regional TV property in a pro-dominated market, not a national brand — but it has a specific, winnable lane (the late window + marquee opponents + owned digital). The strategy is to maximize the controllables, by acknowledging the current brand gap.
